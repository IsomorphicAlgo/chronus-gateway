How it works

Fast pipe, honest numbers

Three jobs, one Rust service. Frames arrive over UDP, get parsed, validated against live orbital state, and streamed out as JSON that mission-control dashboards like NASA Open MCT can consume.

Fast translator

Async, non-blocking ingest (Tokio) parses CCSDS space packets and fans them out over WebSocket without falling behind.

Built-in space map

An SGP4 orbital engine tracks the spacecraft live: azimuth, elevation, range and range-rate from your ground site.

Physics as a sanity check

Doppler, eclipse, link budget, pointing and subsystem checks set physics_flags when telemetry disagrees with physics.
